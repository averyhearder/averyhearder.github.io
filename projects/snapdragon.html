Overview

LumiEdge reimagines outdoor fire features for compact balconies, terraces, and hospitality environments. Designed to integrate with railing systems, its streamlined architectural form introduces warmth and atmosphere while preserving valuable outdoor space.

Looking Back ♡

When we began developing LumiEdge, we wanted to create something that filled a gap rather than simply reimagine an existing fire feature. Looking at small Toronto balconies, we noticed how traditional fire pits and heaters often took up valuable space without offering the same atmosphere as an open flame. Inspired by outdoor torches, we explored integrating a larger fire feature directly into the railing.

Bringing this concept to life through 3D modelling challenged us to think beyond aesthetics, considering materials, heat, durability and the possibility of extending outdoor living into the colder Canadian months. Looking back, we would love to take LumiEdge further through physical prototyping and testing to better understand its performance and practical installation.

behind the bloom ♡

warmth, without losing floor space

On a compact balcony or terrace, every bit of floor counts. Our challenge was bringing in the warmth and atmosphere of a fire without taking that valuable outdoor space away.

So rather than giving the fire its own spot on the floor, we designed LumiEdge to work with the railing. Its slim, streamlined form adds the glow along the edge, and the rest of the space stays open for living.

behind the bloom ♡

building the fire into the railing

Designing a fire feature to live on a railing meant thinking carefully about how it would attach and how everything would fit together.

The exploded drawing tells that story: railing clamps hold LumiEdge in place, with a tempered glass guard, a stainless steel burner, ventilation and an enclosed compartment for the propane cylinder, all within one streamlined form.

behind the bloom ♡

materials, details & settings

We wanted LumiEdge to feel at home in more than one kind of space. We explored GFRC concrete, tempered glass and stainless steel, with samples like Midnight Ash and Sandstone, and worked through details like the railing attachments, ventilation and the enclosed propane system.

Then we pictured it in different settings, from a residential balcony to a hospitality terrace, in both light and dark finishes, to show just how adaptable it could be.
